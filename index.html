FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions

Who can apply for the Creator Program?

Content creators with at least 10,000 followers who make Ros Reborn content on any platform.

What is the minimum follower count?

The follower count required is 10,000 followers.

Can a content creator with multiple platforms apply?

Yes! The more platforms you have, the better. We can reach out and spread the joy of Reborn V2 to more players that way.

How long does application review take?

Requires confirmation from the Ros Reborn team. Do not publish a timeframe until it is confirmed.

What about the perks?

The perks and benefits will be discussed during your interview with us.

Can beginners apply?

As long as you meet the follower criteria, all is fine.

Where will we be promoted?

Promotions will be posted on Ros Reborn's social media platforms and in-game.

Are there any specific platforms that we need to use?

No specific platform is required. Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, and others are all accepted.
